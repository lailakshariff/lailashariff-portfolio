02 · Marketing Strategy

Air Force 1, in a mature market

For my Marketing Management class I analysed Nike's brand marketing for the Air Force 1 — how a performance basketball shoe became a cultural icon — and built a strategy for defending that position against hype fatigue and shifting Gen Z values.

03 · Writing

The Map Inside of Me

Storytelling Accessibility

While designing SayPlay through my work with United Cerebral Palsy, I kept thinking about how design and storytelling could make complex ideas around cerebral palsy more approachable for children. For my Creativity in Business class, I explored that idea through a completely different medium: a children’s book. The Map Inside of Me uses storytelling and illustration to help explain the relationship between the brain and movement in a way that feels imaginative, gentle, and accessible.
