The card titles. Manrope's rounder bowls read friendliest, Satoshi reads like a product, Inter reads like an interface.

Where to look · 03

The tracked uppercase labels and chips. Inter is the closest of the three to Söhne — tighter, more neutral, slightly mechanical.
